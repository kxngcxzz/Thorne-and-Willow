## Spares

`spare-manor-distant.png` is a second hero take, paler and further back.
`spare-after-goldenhour.png` is the rebuilt garden from the patio corner in
warm light. It is a better photograph than `after-garden.png` but it is shot
from a different position, so it cannot go in the slider.
`spare-seasons-contactsheet.png` is the three-up grid the seasons came from.

## Resolution

Everything is 896x1200, which is at or above its display size in ten of the
twelve slots. The hero and the four seasons are the exceptions: the layout
insets them rather than running them edge to edge, so nothing is ever scaled
past its native width. If those five are ever re-rendered at 2K the layout can
go full bleed instead.

## The before and after pair

They share a camera position. Never recrop one without the other or the
slider stops lining up.
